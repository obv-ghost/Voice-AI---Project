From a short recording to a voice that writes back

Three steps, and the first one takes less than a minute.

Give a sample

Record yourself or upload 30 seconds of clear speech in an MP3, WAV or M4A file.

Ilamdar listens

It studies your pitch, pace and the way you pronounce both Urdu and English.

Type and hear

Write in either language, or attach a document, and hear it read back in your voice.

Made for people who write in two scripts

Built around Urdu first, so right-to-left text and Nastaliq feel natural from the first keystroke.

دو زبانیں

Urdu and English together

Switch languages in the middle of a paragraph without changing voice.

لہجہ

Accent and rhythm kept

Your pauses, stress and regional sound carry over, not a generic narrator.

فائلیں

Bring your own material

Attach audio samples, documents to read aloud, or images that contain text.
